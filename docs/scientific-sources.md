# AKM-07 · Risks, hypotheses and next checks

This matrix underpins the experimental preventive investigation for the fictional ODYSSEY-26 mission. The NASA sources explain why the risks and indicators matter. The evidence below consists of observations the prototype can record with the five indicators of the [AKM-06 protocol](akm-06-measurement-protocol.md). Software decisions follow the [canonical v2.0 architecture and the v2.1 addendum](architecture-backend.md).

## Risk and check matrix

**High uncertainty** means the readings allow a useful hypothesis to guide the next collection, with alternative explanations still open. **Not assessable** means missing data, low quality, no temporal alignment, or no suitable indicator. These are documentation labels. In the API, every displayed hypothesis keeps `certainty=unconfirmed`.

| NASA hazard and relevant risk | P0 indicator and observable evidence | Possible hypothesis or explanation | Next check and limitation | Uncertainty and source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Space radiation.** Exposure to ionizing particles and long-term effects. | P0 has no personal dosimetry. The five metrics are not specific to exposure. | Exposure has no hypothesis in the engine. | Context can only be reviewed with external dosimetry records and human assessment by the mission. RR-1 telemetry has its own experimental context. | Not assessable · HRP-H, HRR-96 |
| **Isolation and confinement.** Reduced sleep, circadian misalignment, workload, or changes in well-being. | `sleep_hours` over two distinct periods versus the individual baseline. `mood_score` is an ordinal self-report kept as context. | A shift change, higher workload, or variation in recording can coincide with reduced sleep. | Repeat the sleep record and review collection time and routine. Consider the self-report separately. P0 measures estimated duration and lacks data to infer circadian phase or a psychiatric condition. | High with valid evidence, not assessable without it · HRP-H, HRP-R, HRR-100, HRR-99 |
| **Distance from Earth.** Communication constraints and access to support. | The application has the chronology of measurements and the pending follow-up. It lacks communication telemetry. | A pending follow-up indicates a lack of recorded evidence. The cause of the gap stays open. | Check the local data and hand a summary to the simulated mission's responsible person for human review, as in AKM-04 (investigation flow). Distance supports the decision to keep the flow local. | Not assessable in the engine · HRP-H, HRR-175, HRR-95 |
| **Altered gravity.** Cardiovascular adaptation and other physiological effects. | `heart_rate_bpm` is a single reading in the context of the collection. Reference gravity is mission metadata. | Recent activity, posture, or instrument variation can explain different readings. | Repeat the measurement under comparable conditions, preferably at rest, following AKM-06. A single reading is not enough to attribute an effect to gravity or to characterize cardiovascular risk. | High for collection-related explanations. Gravitational effect not assessable in P0 · HRP-H, HRR-179 |
| **Closed or hostile environment.** Environmental variation in the living space. | `co2_ppm` and `cabin_temp_c` describe cabin air. Use comparable `ok`-quality readings in the windows of interest for the sleep finding. | A CO₂ variation can coincide with reduced sleep. Shared routine, schedules and collection variation are alternatives. | Repeat CO₂ in the same zone and check the time and the instrument quality. If data are missing, prioritize a cabin reading. Temperature is additional context. The cited ISS study concerns reported symptoms, including headache, and lacks evidence to attribute the demo's reduced sleep to CO₂. | High with aligned evidence, not assessable without comparable samples · HRP-H, HRR-100, NASA-CO2 |

## Source catalog and limits of use

The IDs `HRP-H`, `HRP-R` and `HRP-O` are stable local references to the resources listed on the [challenge's Resources tab](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/health-monitoring-software/). In `HRR-*`, the number identifies the risk page in NASA's Human Research Roadmap. The other references use the NASA document identifier or the tool name.

| ID | Reference | Use and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HRP-H | [NASA HRP · Human Spaceflight Hazards](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/) | Taxonomy of the five hazards. Used to give context to exposure in the fictional mission. |
| HRP-R | [NASA HRP · Risks of Human Spaceflight](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/risks/) | Links hazards to the risks the program researches, including health and performance. The categories guide interpretation within the product's scope limits. |
| HRP-O | [NASA HRP · Ongoing HRP Research](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/ongoing-hrp-research/) | Complementary reading on ongoing research. Can accompany the explanation that hypotheses and countermeasures remain subject to research. |
| HRR-100 | [NASA HRR · Sleep Loss, Circadian Desynchronization, and Work Overload](https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=100) | Supports sleep recording and alternative routine hypotheses. | HRR-99 | [NASA HRR · Behavioral Medicine](https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/risks/risk.aspx?i=99) | Provides context for well-being on long missions. The simulation's 1–5 mood is an unvalidated check-in scale. |

## Check against ODYSSEY-26

1. Compute 80% of each simulated baseline. For A1, A2, A3 and A4, the values are 6.40 h, 6.00 h, 6.40 h and 6.24 h. Compare the two nights of the scenario (AKM-04 verification script).
2. A1, A2 and A3 fall at or below their demo reference on both nights. A4 does not meet the rule. With cabin samples of 680 → 950 → 980 ppm in the same window and `ok` quality, the engine can show a temporal coincidence with `certainty=unconfirmed`. The information that reduces the uncertainty is a new habitat CO₂ reading.
3. Repeat the check with CO₂ `suspect`, absent or outside the window. The sleep finding remains supported by its own readings. The environmental comparison has no adequate evidence, and the next check requests or validates the collection needed.
4. Remove the second sleep reading from three profiles. The crew scenario returns `insufficient_data` and asks for the missing information. Technical quality, demonstration of patterns and clinical acceptance are distinct dimensions.

The review for this task covers the relevance of the sources, the coherence of the synthetic scenario and the limits of inference. Approval of the alert criteria and safety texts follows the criteria of AKM-08/09.
