# AKM-09 · ODYSSEY-26 guidance library

Short interface texts for the demonstration, each with context, limit and source. The IDs support review and implementation in AKM-14, AKM-16 and AKM-26 (the IDs are stable identifiers and keep their original Portuguese-derived spelling, for example `MED-SLEEP` for sleep). A Research/Safety review must confirm the texts for educational use. Until that approval, the investigation's `guidance` field stays empty, as the architecture specifies. [AKM-06](akm-06-measurement-protocol.md) · [AKM-08](akm-08-alert-safety.md) · [Architecture](architecture-backend.md)

## Measurement instructions

| ID | Show when | Interface text | Limit and source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MED-SLEEP | Sleep form, with a crew member selected | “Estimate how long you slept during your last completed sleep period. Enter when you woke up and the UTC offset. Leave the reading blank if you do not know the duration.” | Estimated duration from 0 to 24 h. Zero means the person did not sleep. Measures duration only, with no scoring of quality or circadian phase. [AKM-06](akm-06-measurement-protocol.md) · [NASA HRR, sleep](https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=100) |
| MED-HR | Heart-rate form | “Select the crew member. Record one reading from the monitor in bpm and enter the time it was taken. For comparison, take readings under similar conditions, preferably at rest.” | A single positive, finite reading. The app does not verify resting conditions, measurement technique or device accuracy. [AKM-06](akm-06-measurement-protocol.md) · [NASA, wearable device studies](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/wearable-tech-for-space-station-research/) |
| MED-MOOD | Mood form | “How would you rate your emotional well-being now? Choose from 1 (very poor) to 5 (very good).” | Subjective demo check-in. The scale uses 2 = poor, 3 = neutral and 4 = good. Not a validated clinical instrument. [AKM-06](akm-06-measurement-protocol.md) · [NASA HRR, behavioral health](https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/risks/risk.aspx?i=99) |
| MED-CO2 | Habitat CO₂ form or environmental follow-up | “Select Habitat. Enter the cabin air CO₂ reading in ppm, the observation time, and the source. Mark unstable readings as suspect.” | Finite, nonnegative value. Applies only to the simulated mission cabin. A single reading does not classify safety. [AKM-06](akm-06-measurement-protocol.md) · [NASA OCHMO, CO₂](https://www.nasa.gov/ochmo-tb-004-carbon-dioxide-2/) |
| MED-TEMP | Habitat temperature form | “Select Habitat. Record cabin air temperature in °C and the time of the reading.” | Finite value. Cabin interior, distinct from body temperature and from the outside Mars environment. [AKM-06](akm-06-measurement-protocol.md) · [NASA HRP, closed environment](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/) |

For every measurement, the interface asks for an explicit date, time and UTC offset and distinguishes **Manual entry** from **Simulated**. The **NASA historical** origin appears only in the references area. Submission includes the crew member ID for the three individual metrics and uses `null` or omits the ID for habitat metrics. [AKM-03/18 contract](contracts-v2-review.md)

## Statuses and next actions

| ID | Show when | Interface text | Limit and source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EST-NORMAL | Investigation with sufficient data and no pattern in the scope | “The demonstration rule did not identify a pattern {for this profile / for the crew}. See the period and the profiles evaluated.” | Choose the variant by scope. A crew result can coexist with an individual finding. Show the time and coverage. [AKM-08 matrix](akm-08-alert-safety.md) |
| EST-ATENCAO | `attention` with a sleep change | “The demonstration rule found reduced sleep in {profiles}. See the readings and the reference for each profile.” | Fill `{profiles}` with the names in the finding. The rule compares duration with a simulated baseline, with no clinical interpretation. [AKM-08 matrix](akm-08-alert-safety.md) · [NASA HRR, sleep](https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=100) |
| EST-SEM-DADOS | `insufficient_data` | “Comparable readings are still missing to evaluate this scope. {gap}” | Fill `{gap}` with the missing data, for example “Orion's second sleep reading is missing”. Show a new collection only if it is feasible. [AKM-08 matrix](akm-08-alert-safety.md) |
| EST-SUSPEITO | The reading was marked `suspect` | “The reading is in the history as suspect. Check the instrument, the unit and the time before recording a new reading.” | Describes technical quality. The evidence stays outside the rule while suspect. [AKM-06](akm-06-measurement-protocol.md) · [AKM-08 matrix](akm-08-alert-safety.md) |
| EST-COINCIDENCIA | Sleep pattern and CO₂ change in a comparable window | “The CO₂ change occurred in the same period as the reduction in sleep. The relationship between the two observations remains unconfirmed.” | Use with an `unconfirmed` hypothesis and only with the environmental evidence that supports the comparison. [AKM-07 rationale](scientific-sources.md) · [NASA OCHMO, CO₂](https://www.nasa.gov/ochmo-tb-004-carbon-dioxide-2/) |
| EST-SEM-AMBIENTE | Supported sleep finding, with CO₂ absent, suspect or outside the window | “The reduction in sleep was observed. Reliable CO₂ readings are missing for the period, so a coincidence cannot be evaluated.” | Keep the sleep finding. Give the concrete reason for the environmental gap when available. [AKM-08 matrix](akm-08-alert-safety.md) |
| ACAO-CO2 | `next_check.kind=co2_ppm` | “Record a new habitat CO₂ reading. {reanalysis_action}” | Use the target chosen by the engine. The collection can check the environmental pattern. The cause of the human finding remains unconfirmed. AKM-04 flow · [AKM-07 rationale](scientific-sources.md) |
| ACAO-SONO | `next_check.kind=sleep_hours` | “Record the hours slept by {profile} in the next completed period. {reanalysis_action}” | The engine supplies the profile. The window and the next measurement follow the demo rule. [AKM-06](akm-06-measurement-protocol.md) · [AKM-08 matrix](akm-08-alert-safety.md) |
| EST-NOVA | `has_unanalyzed_measurements=true` | “New measurements are available. Update the investigation to include them.” | Show the time of the last analysis, if any. Recording and investigating are separate actions. [AKM-03/18 contract](contracts-v2-review.md) |
| EST-PROTOCOLO | `critical` legend, or a protocol instruction requested by the person | “Consult the mission protocol and contact the designated responsible person. If the protocol calls for immediate referral, follow it now.” | Informational text. `critical` is reserved and the demo engine does not produce it. [AKM-08 matrix](akm-08-alert-safety.md) · [NASA HRR, medical conditions in mission](https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=95) |
| ACAO-REVISAO | Pattern persists after a follow-up, repeated collections fail, or help is requested | “Take the readings, their timestamps and the checks already done to the mission's responsible person for review.” | Manual referral. Mission staff decide the next steps according to the protocol. AKM-04 flow · [AKM-08 matrix](akm-08-alert-safety.md) |
| ERRO-ENTRADA | HTTP 422 when recording a measurement | “Review the indicated field and try saving again.” | Show the field and the reason for the error. Preserve the form content. [AKM-03/18 contract](contracts-v2-review.md) |
| ERRO-ALVO | HTTP 404 for a crew member when recording | “The selected crew member is not available. Refresh the list and select again.” | Preserve the other fields. [AKM-03/18 contract](contracts-v2-review.md) |
| ERRO-REGISTRO-INCERTO | Recording response missing or inconclusive | “The record could not be confirmed. Check whether the measurement appears in the history before sending it again.” | Preserve the form. The write may have completed before the failure. AKM-04 flow |
| ERRO-CARREGAMENTO | Failure to load data or to receive the investigation result | “The data could not be updated. Try again.” | Preserve the last confirmed result and its time, when there is one. The failure does not change the classification. AKM-04 flow |
| ERRO-VERIFICACAO-EVIDENCIA | HTTP 422 when completing the follow-up | “Check the reason shown and select a valid measurement to complete the follow-up.” | Keep the follow-up pending. The link requires a persisted, usable measurement that matches the request and was recorded after it. AKM-04 flow |
| ERRO-VERIFICACAO-AUSENTE | HTTP 404 when completing the follow-up | “The follow-up is not available. Refresh the follow-up list.” | After refreshing, show the state returned by the API. AKM-04 flow |
| ERRO-VERIFICACAO-CONFLITO | HTTP 409 when completing the follow-up | “This follow-up was already completed with a different measurement. Refresh to see the linked record.” | Preserve the existing completion and show the link confirmed by the API. AKM-04 flow |
| ERRO-VERIFICACAO-INCERTA | Completion response missing or inconclusive | “The completion could not be confirmed. Refresh the follow-up before trying again.” | If the API confirms the completion, show the link. If the follow-up is still pending, allow another attempt with the same measurement ID. AKM-04 flow |

Fill `{reanalysis_action}` with “Link the measurement to the pending follow-up and update the investigation.” when an associated follow-up is pending. Without an associated follow-up, use “After saving, update the investigation.” The completion shows the link only after server confirmation. AKM-04 flow

## Message check

| Demo input | Messages and scope |
| --- | --- |
| AKM-28 crew | EST-ATENCAO with Aurora, Orion and Vega. EST-COINCIDENCIA with CO₂ in a comparable window. ACAO-CO2 as the check. |
| AKM-28 individual | EST-ATENCAO for Aurora. ACAO-SONO for the next measurement. The environmental hypothesis is absent because stable CO₂ does not support the comparison. |
| AKM-28 insufficient | EST-SEM-DADOS with the crew sleep gap. Aurora's individual change can be viewed in the matching scope. |
| CO₂ suspect, absent or outside the window | EST-SEM-AMBIENTE accompanies the sleep finding, and EST-SUSPEITO identifies the reading when there is one. The reason determines the available collection. |
| Invalid record or lost response | ERRO-ENTRADA, ERRO-ALVO or ERRO-REGISTRO-INCERTO depending on the outcome of the operation. |
| Follow-up with an incompatible measurement, missing item, conflict or lost response | ERRO-VERIFICACAO-EVIDENCIA (422), ERRO-VERIFICACAO-AUSENTE (404), ERRO-VERIFICACAO-CONFLITO (409) or ERRO-VERIFICACAO-INCERTA depending on the response. |
| Failure to load data or investigation | ERRO-CARREGAMENTO and the last confirmed state, when there is one. |
| Valid follow-up and new measurement | EST-NOVA until the investigation is triggered. ACAO-REVISAO if the pattern persists or help is requested. |

Use the AKM-28 data to reproduce this check. Status texts accompany the investigation result, with reason, icon and time. The app shows the check action only when an applicable next measurement exists. Linking the measurement to the follow-up and the browser sequence are exercised during integration.
